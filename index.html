🧪 Experiment 1: Amplitude Modulation and Demodulation

1. Objective

To study and simulate conventional Amplitude Modulation (DSB-FC), Suppressed Carrier (DSB-SC), and Single Sideband (SSB-SC) waves. To observe their time-domain shapes, calculate modulation parameters, analyze their frequency spectra, introduce AWGN channel noise, and perform diode detection with custom RC loads to verify diagonal clipping and ripple distortion thresholds.

2. Technical Reference & Equations

A. Modulation Schemes:

  • Conventional AM (DSB-FC): The envelope tracks $m(t)$ directly. Requires $m \le 1.0$ for distortionless simple envelope retrieval. sAM(t) = [Ac + Am cos(2π fm t)] cos(2π fc t)
  • Double Sideband Suppressed Carrier (DSB-SC): Suppresses carrier power completely, doubling efficiency. Requires synchronous detection as envelope detector causes severe harmonic distortion. sDSB(t) = Am Ac cos(2π fm t) cos(2π fc t)
  • Single Sideband (SSB-SC): Suppresses carrier and one sideband, saving 50% bandwidth and power. Envelope is flat, requiring coherent detection. sSSB(t) = (AcAm / 2) cos(2π(fc + fm)t)

B. Noise Modeling (AWGN): Additive White Gaussian Noise introduces random fluctuations to the channel signal. The Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) determines noise power relative to signal power. In the time domain, noise voltage follows a normal distribution.

C. Demodulator Circuit Parameters:

To avoid diagonal clipping distortion during diode capacitor discharge, the circuit constants must hold:

RC ≤ √ (1 - m2) / (m · 2π fm)

3. Laboratory Steps

  1. Set the modulation type to Conventional AM. Select the Under Modulation preset. Measure modulation index $m$ and sideband power. Log the values.
  2. Switch the modulation type to DSB-SC. Observe the envelope crossings at zero and notice the phase reversals on the oscilloscope. Check the spectrum analyzer: verify that the carrier spike is gone while the sideband spikes remain.
  3. Switch to SSB-SC. Note the single sideband spike in the spectrum. Observe that the AM wave envelope is completely flat.
  4. Enable the AWGN Channel noise by setting the SNR slider to 15 dB. Notice how noise obscures the carrier wave shapes and raises the noise floor in the spectrum analyser. Check envelope detector performance under noisy channel conditions.
  5. Add several rows to the Observation Table, then click Export Lab Report (PDF).
